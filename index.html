ABOUT GLAMBOOK

Beauty appointments,
made simple.

GlamBook is a front-end salon appointment management prototype created for learning and demonstration.

For customers

Browse services, create an appointment, view saved booking details, and cancel a booking when needed.

For salon staff

Use the admin dashboard to review appointments, update booking statuses, and maintain the service list.

Important information

This project uses browser LocalStorage. Records are limited to the browser and device where they were created. It does not sync between different devices and does not process real payments.
